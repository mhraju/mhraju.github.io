Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Card Manager by MhRaju handles user information. Your privacy and data security are important to us.

Last updated: May 17, 2026

App Name

Card Manager by MhRaju

Overview

Card Manager by MhRaju is designed to help users save, organize, and manage their own card information on their personal device.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your card information. The information you enter in the app is stored locally on your device.

Information We Collect

Card Manager by MhRaju does not collect personal data from users.

The app may allow you to enter card-related information for your own personal use. This information remains stored locally on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Local Data Storage

Any card information saved in the app is stored locally on the user’s mobile device.

  • We do not access your saved card information.
  • We do not upload your saved card information to any server.
  • We do not share your saved card information with third parties.
  • Deleting the app may remove locally stored app data from your device.

Biometric, Face ID, Touch ID, and PIN Security

The app may use device security features such as Face ID, Touch ID, biometric authentication, or PIN login to help protect access to your saved information.

Biometric authentication is handled by your device operating system. Card Manager by MhRaju does not collect, store, or access your biometric data.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third parties.

The app does not provide user card information to advertisers, analytics companies, or external services.

Internet and Server Use

Card Manager by MhRaju is intended to keep user card information on the user’s own device. We do not operate a server to collect or store your card details.

Children’s Privacy

Card Manager by MhRaju is not intended to knowingly collect information from children. Since the app does not collect user data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We encourage users to keep their device secure by using a device passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, or other available security features.

While the app provides local access protection features, users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
