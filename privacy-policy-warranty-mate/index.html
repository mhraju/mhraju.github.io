Privacy Policy

App Name

WarrantyMate by MhRaju

Overview

WarrantyMate by MhRaju is designed to help users save, organize, and manage product warranty information, purchase dates, receipt photos, and warranty card photos on their personal device.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your product, receipt, or warranty information. The information you enter in the app is stored locally on your device.

Information We Collect

WarrantyMate by MhRaju does not collect personal data from users.

The app may allow you to enter product-related information for your own personal use, such as product name, purchase date, warranty end date, reminder time, receipt photo, and warranty card photo. This information remains stored locally on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Receipt and Warranty Photos

WarrantyMate may allow you to capture or upload receipt photos and warranty card photos. These photos are used only inside the app so that you can view and manage your own warranty records.

  • We do not access your uploaded receipt or warranty photos.
  • We do not upload your photos to any server.
  • We do not share your photos with third parties.
  • Your photos remain stored locally on your device unless you export or share them yourself.

Local Data Storage

Any product, warranty, receipt, or reminder information saved in the app is stored locally on the user's mobile device.

  • We do not access your saved warranty information.
  • We do not upload your saved warranty information to any server.
  • We do not share your saved warranty information with third parties.
  • Deleting the app may remove locally stored app data from your device.

Camera and Photo Library Access

WarrantyMate may request access to your device camera or photo library so that you can capture or select receipt and warranty card photos.

Camera and photo access is used only for the purpose of adding images to your warranty records. WarrantyMate does not collect or transmit your photos.

Notifications and Reminders

WarrantyMate may send local notifications to remind you before a product warranty expires. These reminders are scheduled on your device based on the warranty date and reminder time selected by you.

  • Reminder notifications are generated locally on your device.
  • We do not use a server to send warranty reminders.
  • You can control notification permissions from your device settings.

Backup and Export

WarrantyMate may include an export or backup feature that allows you to export your saved product and warranty data as a file for your own use.

Exported files are created only when you choose to export them. You are responsible for where you save, send, or share exported backup files.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third parties.

The app does not provide your receipt, warranty, product, or reminder information to advertisers, analytics companies, or external services.

Internet and Server Use

WarrantyMate is intended to keep your warranty and receipt information on your own device. We do not operate a server to collect or store your warranty records, receipt images, or product details.

Children's Privacy

WarrantyMate by MhRaju is not intended to knowingly collect information from children. Since the app does not collect user data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We encourage users to keep their device secure by using a device passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, biometric authentication, or other available security features.

Since WarrantyMate stores information locally, users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own device and any exported backup files.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
